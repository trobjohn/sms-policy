Privacy Policy

This service collects phone numbers only for the purpose of sending requested SMS alerts and operational notifications. We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in consent data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to deliver the messaging service.

Users may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP, or request help by replying HELP.
